Common Questions

FAQ

How long does a custom gift take?

Most items are completed in 3–5 business days. Complex or bulk orders may take up to 7 days.

Can I order in bulk for events?

Yes! We offer discounts for orders of 10+ items. Contact us on WhatsApp for bulk pricing.

What materials are used?

We primarily use PLA+ and PETG filaments for durability and finish quality. Resin printing is available for fine-detail pieces.

Can I send my own design?

Absolutely! Send your design files or sketches via WhatsApp and we'll provide a custom quote.
