Common Questions

FAQ

How long does production take?

Most masks are completed in 4–7 days depending on finish and complexity. Custom builds may take up to 14 days.

Do you ship outside Cairo?

Yes. We deliver across Egypt and ship internationally, with rates and timing confirmed before your order.

Are the masks wearable?

Many builds include padded interiors, adjustable straps, and ventilation for real comfort during events.

Can you make custom characters?

Yes. Send reference images via WhatsApp and we will provide a quote for your custom build.

What materials do you use?

We primarily use PLA+ and PETG filaments, chosen for strength and finish quality. Resin printing is available for high-detail pieces.
