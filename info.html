SHIPPING & INFO

How mask ordering, production, delivery, care, returns, and privacy work at Forg3d.Art.

How to Order

Browse the mask collection, then use an Order button to open WhatsApp with the design pre-filled. For a new character, send clear reference images and tell us how you plan to wear or display the piece. We confirm the finish, sizing, price, production time, and delivery quotation before work begins.

Production Times

  • Standard masks and helmets: normally 4–7 days, depending on the finish and current workload.
  • Custom character builds: allow up to 14 days after the design and quotation are approved.
  • Complex commissions or multiple-piece builds receive an individual timeline before confirmation.

Need a piece for a specific convention or event date? Tell us when you message so we can confirm whether the deadline is realistic.

Shipping & Delivery

  • Across Egypt: courier delivery is available nationwide, including Cairo, Beni Suef, Alexandria, and other governorates.
  • International orders: shipping is available by quotation. Cost, carrier, timing, and any destination charges are confirmed before you order.
  • Every wearable build is packed to protect its shape, paint, straps, and detailed surfaces.
  • Delivery estimates begin after production is complete and may vary by destination.

Returns & Repairs

Masks and helmets are handcrafted and many are made to order, so approved custom builds are not returnable for a change of mind. We will still put genuine production or delivery problems right:

  • Report damage in transit or a production defect within 48 hours of delivery, with clear photos of the item and packaging.
  • After review, we will arrange the appropriate repair, remake, replacement, or other resolution.
  • Unused, non-custom in-stock pieces may be eligible for exchange within 7 days; return delivery costs and condition requirements are confirmed first.

Mask Care

  • Keep the piece away from high heat, direct sun in a parked car, and open flame.
  • Wipe dust with a soft dry or slightly damp cloth; do not soak the piece or use solvents.
  • Store supported in a cool, dry place so straps, padding, and thin details are not compressed.
  • Contact us before attempting structural or paint repairs so we can recommend the safest method.

Payment

Payment details are arranged directly on WhatsApp when you approve the quotation. Made-to-order and custom builds may require a deposit before production begins. Any remaining balance and delivery charge are confirmed before dispatch.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information needed to discuss, produce, and deliver your order.

  • Order details: your name, contact details, measurements, references, and delivery information are used to fulfil your request and are not sold.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics scripts and cookies are not loaded before you explicitly accept analytics. Declining does not reduce site functionality.
  • Revocation: disabling analytics suppresses future events, updates consent when Analytics has already loaded, and expires known Google Analytics cookies for this site.
  • WhatsApp: messages are also subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy terms.
