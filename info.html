SHIPPING & INFO

How ordering, production, shipping, returns, and your privacy work at Forg3d.Art.

How to Order

All orders are placed personally over WhatsApp. Browse the custom gifts or cosplay masks, then tap any Order or Customize button — it opens a WhatsApp chat with your item pre-filled. Send us your details and we reply with a preview and final quote before anything is printed.

Production Times

  • Custom gifts: most items ready in 3–5 business days.
  • Cosplay masks & helmets: 4–7 days depending on finish; custom builds up to 14 days.
  • Bulk / corporate orders: timelines confirmed per order — contact us for large runs.

Need something urgently? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to prioritise it.

Shipping & Delivery

  • Nationwide (Egypt): we ship to Cairo, Beni Suef, Alexandria, and everywhere in between via trusted courier.
  • Worldwide: we ship to the diaspora — send gifts to family in Egypt, or receive abroad. International rates and timing are confirmed per order.
  • Shipping cost and estimated delivery time are quoted on WhatsApp before you confirm.
  • Every piece is carefully packaged to arrive safely.

Returns & Exchanges

Because most pieces are personalized and made to order, they can't be resold and are non-returnable unless they arrive faulty or damaged. Your satisfaction still matters to us:

  • If an item arrives damaged in transit or has a production defect, contact us within 48 hours with photos and we'll repair, remake, or replace it.
  • We share a preview for approval before printing, so the final piece matches what you signed off on.
  • Non-personalized, in-stock items may be exchanged within 7 days if unused and in original condition.

Payment

Payment details (deposit, cash on delivery, or transfer) are arranged directly on WhatsApp when you confirm your order. Custom and bulk orders may require a deposit before production begins.

Privacy Policy

We keep this simple and only collect what we need to serve you:

  • Order details (name, phone, and what you asked for) are used only to produce and deliver your order. We don't sell or share them.
  • Analytics: this site uses Google Analytics to understand traffic. Analytics cookies load only after you accept them in the consent banner, and you can decline with no loss of functionality.
  • Messaging happens on WhatsApp, which is governed by its own privacy policy.

Questions about your data? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help.
